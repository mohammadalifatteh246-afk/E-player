# E-PLAYER SCREEN-BY-SCREEN UI/UX SPECIFICATION

01 Splash — logo, glow, “Play · Enhance · Experience”.
02 Home — app bar, Continue Watching, Recently Added, categories, favorites, bottom nav.
03 Library — search, filters, media grid/list, scan/add action.
04 Folder — folder header, sorting, grid/list, exclusion action.
05 Search — large search field, recent searches, results, filters.
06 Player — immersive media, back/cast/more, center play, seek bar, audio/subtitles/enhance.
07 Gesture Overlay — temporary brightness, volume, seek, speed and zoom feedback.
08 Playback Settings — decoder, speed, pitch, loop, resume, aspect ratio, gestures.
09 Decoder Mode — Auto, Hardware, Hardware+, Software; show actual decoder backend.
10 Aspect Ratio — Auto/Fit/Fill/Crop/Stretch/Original/16:9/4:3/21:9.
11 Gesture Settings — toggle each gesture, seek interval, sensitivities.
12 Enhancement Studio — AI toggle, Video/Audio tabs, Auto/Manual/Preset, model, resolution, RIFE, Night, 3D, Apply/Preview/Export.
13 Enhancement Manual — strength, denoise, detail, artifact reduction, target, FPS, A/B preview.
14 Enhancement Export — source, preset, target, estimated size, progress, cancel, completion.
15 Audio Track — track, language, codec, channels.
16 Equalizer — five bands, presets, bass, virtualizer, dialogue, night, limiter.
17 Subtitle Track — embedded/external tracks, online search, language, selection.
18 Subtitle Customization — delay, size, colors, outline, position, encoding, preview.
19 Network Stream — URL, advanced headers, open, recent streams, protocol hint.
20 Cast Devices — discover, device cards, connected state, volume, disconnect.
21 Vault — lock status, protected count, import, biometric, auto-lock, storage.
22 Vault Unlock — shield, PIN/biometric, privacy message, lockout state.
23 Trash — deleted items, retention, restore, permanent delete, empty trash.
24 Add Media — Local Files, Scan Library, Network Stream, Select Folder.
25 Scan Progress — folder, files found, thumbnails, progress, cancel, summary.
26 PiP — native PiP transition/restore state, not a fake overlay.
27 Background Playback — lock-screen/media controls-oriented state.
28 2D→3D — source preview, depth, convergence/parallax, left/right, output mode.
29 Model Manager — installed models, task, size, version, runtime, storage, remove.
30 Performance/Hardware — FPS, decoder, AI provider, CPU, memory, GPU, thermal, AI state.
31 General Settings — playback defaults, startup, language, theme, animations.
32 Privacy — vault, diagnostics, subtitle/network permissions, explanations.
33 Storage — library, models, thumbnails, trash, cache.
34 About/Licenses — logo, version, build, acknowledgements.
35 Permission/Capability — why a permission is needed, what remains if denied.

## Quality bar
Every screen must use DESIGN.md, have one primary task, realistic content, safe areas, loading/empty/error states and consistent components.
