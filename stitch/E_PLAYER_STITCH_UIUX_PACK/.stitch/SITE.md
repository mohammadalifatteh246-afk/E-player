# E-PLAYER APP VISION

## Vision
E-Player is a premium offline-first, privacy-oriented multimedia player combining broad playback compatibility, professional controls, media organization, and on-device AI enhancement.

## Product pillars
1. PLAY — local and network playback.
2. CONTROL — gestures, decoder, subtitle, audio, aspect ratio.
3. ENHANCE — AI video/audio processing.
4. ORGANIZE — library, search, folders, history.
5. PROTECT — encrypted vault and recoverable trash.
6. CONNECT — casting and supported network streams.

## Primary navigation
Home / Library / Enhance / Cast / Settings
