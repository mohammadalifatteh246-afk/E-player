# Privacy Policy for E-Player

**Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## 1. Zero Network Connectivity
E-Player is architected from the ground up to be a strictly **offline** media enhancement tool. The application requires zero internet connectivity to function and explicitly does not include any telemetry, analytics, or crash reporting SDKs.

## 2. On-Device Processing
All Artificial Intelligence (AI) operations, including video upscaling (Real-ESRGAN), frame interpolation (RIFE), audio isolation (RNNoise), stereoscopic 3D conversion (Depth Anything), and transcription (ASR), are performed entirely on-device using your hardware's CPU, GPU, or NPU. Your media files, audio data, and exported files never leave your device.

## 3. Storage Permissions
E-Player requests local storage permissions strictly for the purpose of reading the media files you select and writing your exported AI-enhanced media back to your local file system.

## 4. Third-Party Access
Because E-Player operates offline, no data is ever transmitted to us or any third parties.

## 5. Contact
For any concerns regarding this privacy policy, please review the open-source repository and verify the absence of network requests in the architecture.
